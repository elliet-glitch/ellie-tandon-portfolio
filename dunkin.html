MKT 3300: Principles of Marketing

Dunkin' Marketing Plan

A collaborative marketing strategy project focused on consumer analysis, market research, and developing recommendations to strengthen Dunkin's brand strategy.

Tools: Market Research, Consumer Analysis, Microsoft PowerPoint

Timeline: Summer 2026

Role: Marketing Analyst & Team Member

Overview

Working with a five-member team, I developed a comprehensive marketing plan for Dunkin' by analyzing the company's position, target audience, competitors, and opportunities for growth.

Research & Analysis

Our team conducted market research to better understand consumer behavior, brand perception, and competitive positioning. The analysis helped identify strategies related to product offerings, pricing, promotion, and distribution.

Marketing Strategy

I contributed to developing recommendations based on consumer insights, including strategies for improving customer engagement and strengthening brand connections with target audiences.

  • Analyzed target market characteristics and consumer preferences.
  • Evaluated competitor strategies and market opportunities.
  • Contributed to product, price, and place recommendations.

Presentation

The final marketing plan was presented through a professional team presentation, communicating research findings and strategic recommendations.

Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of how research and design decisions work together to create effective user-focused strategies. It also improved my ability to collaborate, communicate ideas, and present solutions.
