Digital Visual Experiment

Quiet
Pixels.

An experimental digital project exploring atmosphere, imagery, composition, and interaction.

Role: Designer

Focus: Visual design and digital interaction

Tools: HTML, CSS, visual design

Project Overview

Quiet Pixels is an experimental project focused on the relationship between imagery and digital interfaces. Instead of treating a website simply as a place to display information, the project explores how layout, space, imagery, and interaction can create a particular feeling.

Design Direction

The visual direction emphasizes simplicity and atmosphere. Large areas of negative space, carefully selected imagery, and restrained typography help create a quieter digital environment.

The project gave me an opportunity to experiment with how visual decisions can influence the way someone moves through a digital experience.
