01 / project editorial design

Editorial / Publication

Yearbook
Design.

A collection of editorial design, photography, layout, and visual storytelling developed through yearbook production.

Role: Associate Editor-in-Chief / Designer

Focus: Editorial design, photography, layout

Tools: Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, Lightroom

Project Overview

Yearbook design was one of my first opportunities to work extensively with visual communication and editorial layout. I worked with other members of the editorial staff to plan page organization, visual direction, and the overall structure of the publication.

The process required balancing creative ideas with deadlines, technical requirements, photography, written content, and collaboration between multiple people.

What I Worked On

  • Editorial page layouts
  • Photography and image selection
  • Typography and visual hierarchy
  • Page planning and organization
  • Photoshop image editing
  • InDesign publication design
  • Collaboration with editorial staff

What I Learned

Yearbook taught me how important collaboration and planning are to creative projects. Because every page contributed to one larger publication, design decisions had to work together rather than exist independently.

It also strengthened my interest in visual storytelling and gave me experience working within real deadlines while maintaining a consistent visual direction.
