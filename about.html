02 / who i am a little about me
01

Hi! I'm Ellie — a Media Arts & Design student at the University of Texas at Dallas, based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

I’m a creative person who enjoys working across different kinds of media. My interests sit at the intersection of design, technology, photography, marketing, and digital experiences. I like taking an idea and figuring out how to turn it into something visual, interactive, and meaningful.

A lot of my experience started with graphic design, photography, and yearbook. Working on yearbook taught me how much I enjoy combining creativity with organization, storytelling, and collaboration. I've designed layouts, photographed events, worked with editorial teams, and helped bring an entire publication together.

Now, I'm expanding those skills into digital design, web experiences, social media, and marketing. I enjoy experimenting with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while also working with tools like Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, and Canva.

I'm especially interested in work where I can combine visual creativity with strategy — whether that's designing a brand, creating content, photographing something, building a website, or figuring out how an idea can connect with an audience.
