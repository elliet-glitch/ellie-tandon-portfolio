I’m a Media Arts + Design student at UT Dallas with an interest in design, photography, digital experiences, and marketing.

I enjoy working on projects where visual communication and technology come together. Whether I’m designing a publication, photographing an event, creating a website, or thinking about a marketing idea, I’m interested in how creative decisions can shape the way people experience information.

My background in yearbook introduced me to editorial design, photography, collaboration, deadlines, and visual storytelling. Since then, I’ve continued exploring those skills through digital and interactive projects.
