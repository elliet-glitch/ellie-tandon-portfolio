About Me

Ellie Tandon

Media Arts and Design student at The University of Texas at Dallas interested in creating digital experiences that combine design, technology, and storytelling.

My Background

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Media Arts and Design with a minor in Marketing and a Certificate in Applied Experience Design and Research at The University of Texas at Dallas.

My interests focus on UX/UI design, interactive websites, creative coding, and digital storytelling. I enjoy creating experiences that are visually engaging while also considering how users interact with technology.

Design Experience

Before studying Media Arts and Design, I developed my visual design skills through yearbook production. As Associate Editor-in-Chief and Graphic Design Editor for Lebanon Trail High School's yearbook, I designed layouts, edited visual content, and collaborated with photographers and writers to create a cohesive publication.

Creative Technology

Through coursework in Internet Studio I, I have explored front-end development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My projects focus on combining technology with storytelling, including interactive narratives and experimental web experiences.

Leadership

As Co-Captain of Lebanon Trail Color Guard, I developed leadership, communication, and teamwork skills by supporting team members, assisting with rehearsals, and helping coordinate performances.
