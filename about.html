I like making things.

I'm a Media Arts & Design student at UT Dallas with an interest in design, marketing, photography, technology, and digital experiences.

I've always enjoyed the process of taking an idea and figuring out how to turn it into something visual. Sometimes that's a graphic, sometimes a photograph, sometimes a video, and sometimes an entire website.

What interests me most is the space between creativity and communication — figuring out not only what something looks like, but why it works and who it's communicating with.
