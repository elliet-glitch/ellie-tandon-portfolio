02 / who i am a little about me
01

Hi! I'm Ellie — a Media Arts & Design student at the University of Texas at Dallas, based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

I’m interested in the space where design, technology, photography, marketing, and digital experiences meet. I like creating things that communicate an idea visually, but I also enjoy thinking about the strategy and purpose behind what I'm making.

My creative background started with graphic design, photography, and yearbook. Through yearbook, I worked on page layouts, visual storytelling, photography, and editorial planning while collaborating with other students to bring an entire publication together.

Since then, I’ve expanded into digital work. I enjoy experimenting with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and building interactive websites and experiences. I also work with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Lightroom, After Effects, Canva, Figma, and other creative tools.

I'm especially interested in creative opportunities involving design, branding, social media, content creation, photography, marketing, UX, and digital experiences. I like projects where I can make something, collaborate with other people, and keep learning along the way.
